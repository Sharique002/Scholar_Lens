Everything a research team works with

Each module shares the same profiles, permissions and data, so nothing is entered twice.

Publications

Upload PDFs or import records, detect duplicates and match authors to researcher profiles.

Researcher directory

Verified profiles with departments, interests, skills and ORCID or Scopus identifiers.

Expertise search

Find people by topic, technique or skill, with filters for department and availability.

Projects & milestones

Plan milestones, track progress on a timeline and get warned when work is at risk.

Research openings

Post assistantships and positions; applicants are matched on the skills each opening needs.

Facilities & bookings

See weekly availability, request time on shared equipment and sync bookings to your calendar.

Funding calls

Browse open calls with deadline countdowns, watch calls and track applications.

Collaboration

Suggested collaborators with the reasons behind each match, requests and a network graph.

Reports

Academic-year reports on publications, funding and facility use, exportable as CSV.

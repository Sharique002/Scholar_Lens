Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Scholar Lens ("we", "our", or "us"). Scholar Lens is a research intelligence and academic collaboration platform designed to empower researchers, faculty, and institutions. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

When you register or sign in using Google OAuth2 Single Sign-On, we collect:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and profile picture provided by your Google account.
  • Academic Profile Data: Research papers, institutional affiliation, department, and research interests you voluntarily submit.
  • Platform Usage: Activity logs, paper evaluations, collaboration requests, and facility bookings within the platform.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and provide secure single sign-on access.
  • Generate and display your academic profile and researcher directory listing.
  • Facilitate research intelligence, novelty scoring, and interdisciplinary collaboration matching.
  • Notify you about relevant funding opportunities, milestones, and shared facility schedules.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Scholar Lens's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your Google account data or academic research data to third parties.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You may request account deletion or the removal of your personal and academic data at any time by contacting us at stargroupssk@gmail.com.

If you have questions or concerns regarding this policy, please reach out to us at:
